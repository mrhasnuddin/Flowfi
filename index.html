FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is FlowFi?

FlowFi is decentralized infrastructure for the institutional management of on-chain capital. It runs two treasuries: a Governance Treasury for rules and consensus, and an Investor Treasury for audited capital deployment.

What does the Governance Treasury do?

It manages protocol parameters, voting weights and the FF consensus mechanism. 1.2B FF is locked in it.

What does the Investor Treasury do?

It grows capital through audited deployment and incubation. It holds $10M+ in seed capital.

How many FF tokens will exist?

FF is capped at 1 Billion. There is no inflation and no minting.

What is the Black Hole Launch?

The Black Hole Launch is FlowFi's fair launch distribution. It is 100% transparent, has no VC pre-allocations, and sends 50% to ecosystem incentives and builder grants.

How does FlowFi stay solvent?

An over-collateralized ratio keeps the protocol solvent and stable.

Is FlowFi audited?

Yes. FlowFi is Certik audited.

What is on FlowFi's roadmap?

Three phases: System Launch (Genesis Governance, active), Expansion (Backflow Optimization, in progress) and Global Tier (Enterprise Scaling, planned). Together they move capital to the ENI Mainnet.
